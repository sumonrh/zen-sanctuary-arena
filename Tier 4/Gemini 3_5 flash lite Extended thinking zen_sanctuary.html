Zen Sanctuary Voxel Experience

Explore a tranquil Minecraft-style Japanese garden built with variable voxel sizes (from 4x4 mountain blocks down to 0.25x0.25 micro-voxels for samurai armor and animals).

• Pagoda Temple & Torii Gate: Multi-tiered wooden sanctuary with warm shoji paper screens and glowing lantern lighting.

• Animated Life: Samurai guardians with idle breathing, roaming sheep and alpacas, and soaring flocks of birds.

• Dynamic Time Modes: Switch seamlessly between Morning Sun, Sunset, and Night mode.

• Web Audio Synthesizer: Ambient wind and gentle random wind chimes.
