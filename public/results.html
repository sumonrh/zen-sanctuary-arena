The least-compared simulations. If a contender has very few matches, treat its rank as provisional.

Method. Bradley–Terry pairwise model with a weak L2 prior. Ties count as half a win; skips are excluded; a reported failure counts as a loss for the failed sim. Ratings are shown on an Elo-style scale (1500 ≈ the middle). Confidence intervals and P(#1) come from bootstrapping the ballots. The family table refits the model with same-family matchups removed, so it answers “which model” rather than “which setting”.
