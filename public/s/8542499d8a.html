THE LIGHT BETWEEN THE LEAVES

A moment of komorebi.

Komorebi is the Japanese word for sunlight filtering through trees. This little sanctuary is an invitation to notice it: the cherry blossoms drifting, the sheep grazing, the lanterns coming to life.

Everything here is built from blocks, from the snow on the mountains to the golden crescents on the guardians' helmets. Even the wind and chimes are made here, synthesized gently in your browser.
