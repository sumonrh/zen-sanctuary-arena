A SMALL GUIDE TO KOMOREBI

There is no hurry here.

A three-tiered pagoda rests between cherry blossoms and snow-capped peaks. Cross the red bridge, meet the samurai guardians, or follow the sheep and alpacas into the meadow.

Every texture, tree, and creature is made here, from pixels and blocks. The soundscape is synthesized live. No recordings. No destination. Just a little space to be.
