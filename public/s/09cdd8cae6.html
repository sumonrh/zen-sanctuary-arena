A WORLD WITH NO TO-DO LIST

Sunlight through
the leaves.

That's komorebi: the Japanese word for the light that finds its way through trees. This imagined mountain sanctuary is a small invitation to notice, wander, and do a little less.

Every beam, blossom, and snowy peak is built from blocks. Guardians breathe, sheep graze, petals fall. The world keeps its own gentle rhythm.

This single-file world needs WebGL and an internet connection for Three.js, Tailwind, and fonts. All scenery, textures, animation, and sound are generated here.
