About this world

Everything is built from boxes: 4×4 mountain terrain, 2×2 ground, 1×1 architecture and 0.25×0.25 micro-voxels for samurai armour, sheep, alpacas, birds and ornaments.

Art is procedurally painted onto 16×16 canvases and sampled with NearestFilter for crisp pixels.
